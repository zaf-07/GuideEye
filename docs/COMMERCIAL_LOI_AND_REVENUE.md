### Background:
Client supports blind, low-vision, and hard-of-hearing university students and campus visitors who require hands-free navigation, accessible document comprehension (clinic bills, academic paperwork, room schedules), and direct peer-to-peer communication across sensory barriers. Provider has developed GuideEye, an ambient assistive system integrating on-device spatial vision, wearable voice input, ElevenLabs natural narration, and HarnessRouter Universal Harness Protocol (UHP) autonomous document errand execution.

### Terms of Pilot Deployment:
1. **Scope of Pilot:** Provider will deploy GuideEye across ten (10) active student pilot participants on campus for a duration of three (3) months starting December 1, 2026.
2. **Commitment Fee & Structure:**
   - **Pilot Rate:** $49.00 USD per active wearer per month.
   - **Total Committed Seats:** 10 Seats.
   - **Total Pilot Value:** **$1,470.00 USD** (Invoice net-30, billing upon onboarding).
3. **Included Capabilities:**
   - Full access to on-device YOLO hazard detection and turn-by-turn walking navigation.
   - Real-time Deaf-Blind Bridge (camera sign recognition, 3D ASL sign avatar, and live streaming captions).
   - Autonomous document reading and errand action planning powered by HarnessRouter (`chrn_e00fb0a783d07115989457c64f6364d0`).
   - Private, locally-encrypted memory storage with optional Supermemory synchronization.
4. **Safety & Compliance:** Provider guarantees that all financial transactions and external document actions require explicit spoken confirmation ("Yes") from the wearer and comply with FERPA / HIPAA privacy isolation standards.
