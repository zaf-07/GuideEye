What it does

See. Remember. Act.

Today’s vision apps make you aim a phone and tap buttons — then forget everything the moment you close them. GuideEye works the other way round.

See

Live hazard warnings spoken as you move: “Chair ahead, left.” Detection runs locally, so it stays fast and private.

Remember

Ask later: “Where did I leave my keys?” GuideEye links what it sees into a graph — keys on the counter, counter next to the kettle — so it can answer from the connections, not just the words.

Act

Say “Buy me a coffee.” It reads the item and price aloud, then pays only after you say yes.

Hands-free by design

Voice-first controls keep the phone accessible while it stays clipped to your chest. Your hands stay free for your cane, your dog, your day.

Bridge Coming soon

A bridge between deaf and blind people: sign language voiced aloud, and speech shown as large captions.

Made for everyday devices

GuideEye is an accessibility layer for the phone and computer you already use.

How it works

Your voice is the command. The phone is your eyes.

Safety-critical detection runs locally and privately. Reasoning and natural voice use the cloud.

  1. 1

    You talk

    Speak through your phone or browser microphone. A chest-clipped phone camera watches the way ahead.

  2. 2

    It understands

    Hazards are spotted on-device in real time. An AI agent handles questions, graph memory and requests.

  3. 3

    It answers — or asks

    You hear a clear spoken reply. For anything that matters, like paying, it waits for your “yes”.

    GuideEye service

    Options

    Voice replies are on.

    Camera frames go to the GuideEye service. When you ask a question, that single frame is sent to the configured cloud vision provider for the answer. Directions send your location to OpenStreetMap services. Speech is recognised by your browser. Nothing is recorded. Payments are test-mode only.

    Built for trust

    Nothing happens behind your back.

    An assistant you can’t see has to earn your confidence. GuideEye is designed around three promises.

    • Private by default

      No raw video or audio is stored.

    • You confirm

      Money never moves without a spoken yes.

    • Honest about doubt

      When it isn’t sure, it says so instead of guessing.

    Please note: GuideEye is a prototype. It does not replace a white cane, guide dog, orientation training or emergency services.
